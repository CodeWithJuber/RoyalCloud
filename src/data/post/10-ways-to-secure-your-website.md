Website security isn't only a concern for big companies. Automated bots probe millions of sites every day looking for weak passwords, outdated software and unpatched vulnerabilities. The good news: a handful of straightforward habits will block the vast majority of attacks. Here's a practical checklist.

## 1. Install SSL everywhere

An SSL certificate encrypts traffic between your visitors and your server, and it's a confirmed SEO ranking factor. Every Royal Clouds plan includes a **free, auto-renewing SSL certificate**, there's no reason to run plain HTTP anymore.

## 2. Keep everything updated

Outdated CMS cores, themes and plugins are the number one cause of website compromises. Enable automatic updates where you can, and review them regularly where you can't.

## 3. Use strong, unique passwords

Reused passwords are catnip for attackers. Use a password manager, make every login unique, and never share admin credentials over email.

## 4. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)

Even if a password leaks, 2FA stops attackers from logging in. Turn it on for your hosting account, control panel and CMS admin.

## 5. Limit login attempts

Brute-force attacks guess passwords thousands of times per minute. Rate-limiting login attempts (and changing default admin URLs) shuts them down quickly.

## 6. Run a web application firewall (WAF)

A WAF filters malicious traffic before it ever reaches your site. Combined with Cloudflare's network-level protection, it blocks common exploits like SQL injection and cross-site scripting.

## 7. Take regular backups, and test them

A backup you've never restored is just a hope. Royal Clouds takes **automatic remote backups** so you can roll back your entire site in one click after any incident.

## 8. Scan for malware

Automated malware scanning catches injected code and suspicious files early. Our managed plans include scanning and antivirus that respond to threats instantly.

## 9. Lock down file permissions

Misconfigured permissions let attackers write to files they shouldn't. Stick to sensible defaults (typically `644` for files and `755` for folders) and never use `777`.

## 10. Choose a secure host

Your hosting provider is your first line of defence. Look for DDoS protection, hardened firewalls, regular security audits and 24/7 monitoring, all standard on Royal Clouds infrastructure.

## A quick monthly routine

- Check for and apply updates.
- Confirm backups ran and test a restore occasionally.
- Review user accounts and remove ones you no longer need.
- Glance at access logs for anything unusual.
